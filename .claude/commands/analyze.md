Use the **analyze-agent** and **analysis skill** to perform deep, systematic analysis.

## Task

Analyze: $ARGUMENTS

## Process

1. **Clarify the analysis target**:
   - Restate what's being analyzed
   - Identify key questions to answer
   - Define scope and success criteria

2. **Decompose into dimensions**:
   - Technical (architecture, code quality, performance, security)
   - Business (value, cost, ROI, strategic fit)
   - Risk (what could go wrong, likelihood, impact)
   - User Impact (experience, adoption, satisfaction)
   - Operational (maintenance, support, monitoring)

3. **Investigate systematically**:
   - Gather evidence from code, docs, data
   - Document findings with file:line references
   - Identify patterns and trends
   - Note cross-dimensional relationships

4. **Evaluate options**:
   - Identify 2-4 realistic approaches
   - Compare pros/cons for each
   - Create comparison table
   - Highlight key differentiators

5. **Synthesize insights**:
   - Extract 3-5 key findings
   - Make specific recommendations
   - Prioritize actions (critical, high, medium, low)
   - Document assumptions and unknowns

6. **Deliver structured output**:
   - Executive summary (2-3 sentences)
   - Key findings with evidence
   - Options comparison table
   - Specific recommendations with rationale
   - Action checklist

## Output Format

Provide a comprehensive analysis report including:
- **Executive Summary**: Brief overview
- **Context & Goal**: What's being analyzed and why
- **Key Findings**: 3-5 most important insights with evidence
- **Detailed Analysis**: By dimension (technical, business, risk, etc.)
- **Options Comparison**: Table comparing 2-4 approaches
- **Risks & Gaps**: What's missing, unclear, or concerning
- **Recommendations**: Prioritized next steps with justification
- **Action Checklist**: Concrete steps to take

## Quality Standards

- Evidence-based: All claims backed by file:line references or data
- Multi-dimensional: Consider technical, business, risk, user, operational aspects
- Balanced: Present multiple perspectives and trade-offs
- Actionable: Specific recommendations with clear next steps
- Honest: Explicit about unknowns and assumptions
