Use the **proofread-agent** and **proofreading skill** for professional editing.

## Task

Proofread and edit: $ARGUMENTS

## Process

1. **Understand content**:
   - Identify content type and audience
   - Determine purpose and formality level
   - Check style guidelines (CLAUDE.md, project docs)
   - Do initial read-through for overall assessment

2. **Apply comprehensive editing checklist**:

   **A. Grammar & Mechanics**:
   - Subject-verb agreement
   - Verb tense consistency
   - Pronoun-antecedent agreement
   - Article usage (a/an/the)
   - Punctuation (commas, periods, semicolons, apostrophes)
   - Capitalization
   - Spelling
   - Homophones (their/there/they're, its/it's, your/you're)

   **B. Clarity & Readability**:
   - Active voice preferred
   - Concise language (remove redundancy)
   - Strong, specific verbs
   - Short sentences (15-20 words average)
   - Appropriate paragraph length (3-5 sentences)
   - Clear transitions
   - Jargon defined or simplified
   - Ambiguity eliminated

   **C. Structure & Flow**:
   - Logical order of ideas
   - Descriptive headings with consistent hierarchy
   - One main idea per paragraph
   - Effective use of lists
   - Clear introduction and conclusion
   - Consistent formatting

   **D. Tone & Style**:
   - Consistent tone throughout
   - Brand voice maintained (if applicable)
   - Appropriate person (first/second/third)
   - Consistent use of contractions
   - Inclusive language
   - Professional and appropriate

   **E. Consistency**:
   - Same terms for same concepts
   - Consistent spelling variant (US/UK English)
   - Consistent date and number formats
   - Consistent hyphenation
   - Abbreviations defined at first use
   - Parallel structure in lists

3. **Deep clarity pass**:
   - Convert passive to active voice
   - Eliminate nominalizations
   - Remove redundancy
   - Strengthen weak constructions
   - Replace vague words with specific ones
   - Simplify complex sentences

4. **Track changes**:
   - Count changes by category
   - Document major edits with before/after
   - Note rationale for significant changes
   - Build change summary

5. **Final validation**:
   - Read edited version start to finish
   - Check flow and coherence
   - Verify meaning preserved
   - Confirm all errors corrected
   - Validate against requirements

6. **Deliver with summary**:
   - Present edited content
   - Provide change summary by category
   - Highlight major changes
   - Suggest optional improvements

## Output Format

Provide complete editing package including:
- **Editing Summary**: Type, audience, length, total changes, date
- **Edited Content**: Polished, publication-ready text
- **Change Summary**: Changes by category (grammar, clarity, style, structure)
- **Major Changes**: Significant edits with before/after and rationale
- **Optional Improvements**: Suggestions for author's consideration
- **Quality Metrics**: Correctness, clarity, consistency, tone, preservation

## Quality Standards

- Correct: No grammar, spelling, or punctuation errors
- Clear: Easy to understand, no ambiguity
- Consistent: Uniform style and terminology throughout
- Appropriate: Tone matches audience and purpose
- Preserved: Author's voice and intent maintained
- Transparent: All significant changes documented
