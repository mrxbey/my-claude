Analyze competitors systematically and generate strategic recommendations.

## Task

Competitive analysis for: $ARGUMENTS

## Multi-Phase Competitive Analysis Workflow

### Phase 1: Competitor Identification (analyze-agent)

**Use analyze-agent** to map competitive landscape:

1. **Direct Competitors**: Same product, same market, same customer
2. **Indirect Competitors**: Different product, same need/outcome
3. **Emerging Threats**: New entrants, disruptors, substitute products
4. **Adjacent Players**: Could enter your market

**Deliverable**: Competitor list (5-10) with categorization

**Quality Gate**: Comprehensive competitor set identified?

---

### Phase 2: Information Gathering

**Collect data on each competitor**:

1. **Product/Service**: Features, capabilities, limitations
2. **Pricing**: Tiers, discounts, value propositions
3. **Market Position**: Market share, growth rate, customer base
4. **Strengths**: What they do well, competitive advantages
5. **Weaknesses**: Gaps, customer complaints, limitations
6. **Strategy**: Positioning, messaging, target audience

**Sources**:
- Company websites, pricing pages
- Product documentation
- Customer reviews (G2, Trustpilot, app stores)
- News articles, press releases
- Social media, thought leadership
- Industry reports

**Deliverable**: Competitor profiles with data

**Quality Gate**: Sufficient data for comparison?

---

### Phase 3: Comparison Framework (analyze-agent)

**Create structured comparison**:

1. **Feature Comparison Matrix**:

| Feature | Us | Competitor A | Competitor B | Competitor C |
|---------|----|--------------|--------------|--------------| 
| Feature 1 | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Feature 2 | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Feature 3 | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |

2. **Pricing Comparison**:
   - Entry price point
   - Mid-tier pricing
   - Enterprise pricing
   - Pricing model (per user, per feature, flat)

3. **Market Position Comparison**:
   - Market share estimate
   - Growth trajectory
   - Customer segments
   - Geographic presence

**Deliverable**: Comparison matrices and charts

**Quality Gate**: Fair, data-driven comparison?

---

### Phase 4: SWOT Analysis (analyze-agent + analysis skill)

**For each major competitor, conduct SWOT**:

**Strengths**:
- What advantages do they have?
- What resources/capabilities are strong?
- What do customers praise?

**Weaknesses**:
- What limitations exist?
- What do customers complain about?
- What gaps in offering?

**Opportunities** (for them):
- What market trends favor them?
- What could they exploit?
- Where could they expand?

**Threats** (to them):
- What challenges do they face?
- What could disrupt them?
- What regulatory/market risks?

**Deliverable**: SWOT for each key competitor

**Quality Gate**: Balanced, evidence-based SWOT?

---

### Phase 5: Strategic Positioning

**Identify where you fit**:

1. **Positioning Map**:
   - Axes: Price (low→high) × Features (basic→advanced)
   - Plot yourself and competitors
   - Identify gaps and clustering

2. **Differentiation Analysis**:
   - Where are you unique?
   - Where are you similar?
   - Where are you behind?
   - White space opportunities?

3. **Competitive Advantages**:
   - What do you do better?
   - What's defensible?
   - What's temporary?

4. **Vulnerabilities**:
   - Where are you exposed?
   - Who threatens you most?
   - What could you lose?

**Deliverable**: Positioning map and strategic analysis

**Quality Gate**: Clear positioning identified?

---

### Phase 6: Strategic Recommendations

**Generate actionable insights**:

1. **Defend**: Where to strengthen current position
2. **Attack**: Where to take market share from competitors
3. **Expand**: Where to move into new spaces (white space)
4. **Watch**: Emerging threats to monitor

**Recommendations Format**:
- **Action**: What to do
- **Why**: Based on which insight
- **Impact**: Expected outcome
- **Effort**: Resource requirement
- **Priority**: High/Medium/Low
- **Timeline**: When to execute

**Deliverable**: Prioritized strategic recommendations

**Quality Gate**: Recommendations actionable and prioritized?
