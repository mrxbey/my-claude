## Multi-Phase Content Creation Workflow

This workflow guides content creation from concept to publication-ready copy, ensuring quality, consistency, and alignment with brand voice.

### Phase 1: Audience & Purpose Definition (analyze-agent)

**Use analyze-agent** to clarify content goals:

1. **Define Target Audience**:
   - Who will read this? (job role, industry, expertise level)
   - What do they care about? (pain points, goals, interests)
   - What do they already know? (baseline knowledge)
   - What language resonates? (technical, business, casual)

2. **Clarify Content Purpose**:
   - **Inform**: Educate audience on a topic
   - **Persuade**: Convince readers to take action
   - **Entertain**: Engage and delight
   - **Convert**: Drive specific business outcome

3. **Define Success Metrics**:
   - How will we measure success? (views, shares, leads, conversions)
   - What action do we want readers to take?
   - What's the primary call-to-action (CTA)?

4. **Determine Tone & Style**:
   - Formality level: Professional / Conversational / Technical
   - Voice: Authoritative / Friendly / Provocative / Educational
   - Perspective: First person (we/I) / Second person (you) / Third person
   - Brand voice: How does our brand speak?

**Deliverable**: Content brief with audience, purpose, success metrics, tone

**Quality Gate 1**: Content goals clear?
- [ ] Target audience defined
- [ ] Primary purpose identified
- [ ] Success metrics set
- [ ] Tone and style determined

---

### Phase 2: Research & Outline

**Research topic and create structure**:

1. **Topic Research** (use research-synthesis workflow if needed):
   - What's been written about this topic?
   - What angles are overdone? What's unique?
   - What questions does audience have?
   - What examples/data exist?

2. **Key Points Identification**:
   - What 3-5 main points support your purpose?
   - What evidence backs each point?
   - What examples illustrate each point?
   - What objections need addressing?

3. **Create Outline**:
   - **Headline** (working title - will refine later)
   - **Hook** (opening that grabs attention)
   - **Introduction** (set context, preview main points)
   - **Body** (3-5 sections, each making one key point)
   - **Conclusion** (summarize, reinforce message)
   - **Call-to-Action** (what reader should do next)

4. **Gather Supporting Materials**:
   - Statistics and data
   - Expert quotes
   - Examples and case studies
   - Visuals/diagrams (note what's needed)
   - Links to references

**Deliverable**: Detailed outline with supporting materials

**Quality Gate 2**: Outline solid?
- [ ] 3-5 clear main points
- [ ] Logical flow
- [ ] Supporting evidence identified
- [ ] Hook and CTA defined

---

### Phase 3: Draft Creation

**Write first draft following outline**:

1. **Write the Hook** (first paragraph):
   - Start with: Question, surprising stat, bold claim, story, problem statement
   - Make it relevant to audience
   - Promise value (what they'll learn/gain)
   - Keep it concise (2-3 sentences)

2. **Write Introduction**:
   - Expand on hook
   - Provide context/background
   - Preview main points (roadmap)
   - Set expectations for length/depth

3. **Write Body Sections**:
   For each main point:
   - **Topic sentence**: State the point clearly
   - **Evidence**: Data, research, expert quotes
   - **Explanation**: Why this matters, how it works
   - **Example**: Concrete illustration
   - **Transition**: Connect to next point

4. **Write Conclusion**:
   - Summarize main points (briefly)
   - Reinforce key message
   - Answer "so what?" (why this matters)
   - Future implications (where this is heading)

5. **Craft Call-to-Action**:
   - Specific action (download, sign up, read more, share)
   - Clear benefit (what they get)
   - Low friction (easy to do)

6. **Add Subheadings**:
   - Break up text with H2/H3 subheadings
   - Make them descriptive and scannable
   - Use parallel structure

**Deliverable**: Complete first draft

**Quality Gate 3**: Draft complete?
- [ ] All sections written
- [ ] Follows outline
- [ ] Appropriate length
- [ ] Subheadings added

---

### Phase 4: Style & Tone Refinement

**Ensure style matches audience and brand**:

1. **Read Aloud Test**:
   - Read content aloud (or have it read to you)
   - Does it sound natural?
   - Is pacing good? (mix of long/short sentences)
   - Are transitions smooth?

2. **Tone Check**:
   - Is formality level appropriate for audience?
   - Is voice consistent throughout?
   - Does it match brand guidelines?
   - Is it engaging but not over-the-top?

3. **Clarity Assessment**:
   - Can a reader understand without re-reading?
   - Are complex ideas explained simply?
   - Is jargon defined or avoided?
   - Are sentences concise?

4. **Engagement Elements**:
   - **Vary sentence length**: Mix short punchy sentences with longer complex ones
   - **Active voice**: "We analyzed" not "Analysis was conducted"
   - **Concrete language**: "Increased revenue by $50K" not "Improved performance"
   - **Rhetorical devices**: Questions, comparisons, contrasts
   - **Personality**: Don't be bland - let voice come through

5. **Format for Readability**:
   - Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences)
   - Bullet points for lists
   - Bold for key terms
   - Quotes for important insights
   - White space for breathing room

**Deliverable**: Style-refined draft

**Quality Gate 4**: Style appropriate?
- [ ] Tone matches audience
- [ ] Voice consistent
- [ ] Clear and concise
- [ ] Engaging and readable

---

### Phase 5: Proofreading (proofread-agent)

**Use proofread-agent** for comprehensive editing:

1. **Grammar & Punctuation**:
   - Spelling errors
   - Grammar mistakes
   - Punctuation consistency
   - Capitalization rules

2. **Clarity & Structure**:
   - Unclear sentences
   - Awkward phrasing
   - Logical flow
   - Transition quality

3. **Consistency**:
   - Terminology usage
   - Style guide adherence
   - Formatting consistency
   - Brand voice

4. **Conciseness**:
   - Remove redundancy
   - Cut unnecessary words
   - Tighten sentences
   - Eliminate filler

5. **Fact-Checking**:
   - Verify statistics
   - Check claims
   - Validate links
   - Confirm attributions

**Deliverable**: Proofread draft with corrections

**Quality Gate 5**: Error-free?
- [ ] No grammar/spelling errors
- [ ] All facts verified
- [ ] Style guide compliant
- [ ] Links validated

---

### Phase 6: Compliance & Brand Check (docs-agent)

**Use docs-agent** to verify brand compliance:

1. **Brand Guidelines Check**:
   - Voice and tone match brand guide?
   - Terminology consistent with brand vocabulary?
   - Messaging aligned with brand positioning?
   - Legal disclaimers included (if required)?

2. **Content Policy Compliance**:
   - No prohibited topics/language?
   - Inclusive language used?
   - Accessibility considerations met?
   - Sensitive topics handled appropriately?

3. **SEO Compliance** (if applicable):
   - Keywords naturally incorporated?
   - Meta description drafted?
   - Headers structured for SEO?
   - Links follow best practices?

4. **Legal Review** (if needed):
   - Claims substantiated?
   - Competitor names used appropriately?
   - Copyright considerations?
   - Regulatory compliance?

**Deliverable**: Compliance verification report

**Quality Gate 6**: Compliant?
- [ ] Brand guidelines met
- [ ] Content policies followed
- [ ] SEO optimized (if applicable)
- [ ] Legal review passed (if required)

---

### Phase 7: Final Polish

**Perfect headlines, formatting, CTAs**:

1. **Headline Optimization**:
   - Write 5-10 headline variations
   - Test for: Clarity, Curiosity, Value proposition, Keyword inclusion
   - Choose best one or A/B test
   - Ensure it delivers on promise

2. **Opening Hook Refinement**:
   - First sentence: Grab attention
   - Make reader want to continue
   - Set up value proposition

3. **Subheading Enhancement**:
   - Make scannable (can understand from subheadings alone)
   - Include keywords
   - Consistent formatting
   - Parallel structure

4. **CTA Strengthening**:
   - Action-oriented verb (Download, Start, Join, Learn)
   - Benefit clear (Get instant access, Save 10 hours, etc.)
   - Urgent but not pushy
   - Visually distinct

5. **Final Format Check**:
   - Proper HTML/Markdown formatting
   - Images placed correctly (with alt text)
   - Links opening correctly
   - Mobile-friendly
   - Print-friendly (if applicable)

**Deliverable**: Publication-ready content

**Quality Gate 7**: Polished and ready?
- [ ] Headline compelling
- [ ] Hook grabs attention
- [ ] CTA clear and strong
- [ ] Formatting perfect
- [ ] All media assets ready

## Content Type Templates

### Blog Post (800-1500 words)

**Structure**:
1. Hook (problem or question)
2. Context (why this matters now)
3. Main points (3-5 sections with subheadings)
4. Conclusion (summary and future)
5. CTA (read more, subscribe, download)

**Tone**: Conversational, helpful, engaging

---

### Whitepaper (2000-5000 words)

**Structure**:
1. Executive summary (1 page)
2. Problem statement
3. Background/context
4. Solution/approach
5. Evidence (data, case studies)
6. Conclusion
7. Recommendations
8. About company/author

**Tone**: Professional, authoritative, data-driven

---

### Marketing Copy (100-500 words)

**Structure**:
1. Attention-grabbing headline
2. Problem identification
3. Solution introduction
4. Benefits (3-5 bullet points)
5. Social proof (testimonial, stats)
6. Strong CTA

**Tone**: Persuasive, benefit-focused, urgent

---

### Article (1000-2000 words)

**Structure**:
1. News hook or trend
2. Why it matters
3. Analysis (multiple perspectives)
4. Expert insights
5. Implications
6. What's next

**Tone**: Journalistic, balanced, informative

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Write for ONE person**: Imagine your ideal reader and write to them
2. **Show, don't tell**: Use examples and stories, not just abstract concepts
3. **Cut ruthlessly**: First drafts are always too long - edit for concision
4. **Lead with value**: Every paragraph should give the reader something useful
5. **Break it up**: Use subheadings, bullets, quotes to create visual breaks
6. **End strong**: Conclusion should be memorable, not just a rehash
7. **Test headlines**: The headline is 80% of your success - spend time on it
8. **Get feedback**: Have someone in target audience read before publishing
9. **Optimize for skimming**: Most readers scan - make it work when skimmed
10. **Iterate**: Content can be updated - don't let perfect be enemy of good

## Quality Standards

This workflow ensures:
- **Audience-focused**: Written for reader, not writer
- **Purposeful**: Every piece has clear goal and CTA
- **Evidence-based**: Claims backed by data and examples
- **Professional**: Error-free, polished, branded
- **Engaging**: Keeps reader interested throughout
- **Actionable**: Reader knows what to do next
