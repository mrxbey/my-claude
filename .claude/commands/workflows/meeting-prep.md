Prepare thoroughly for important meetings with research, talking points, and success planning.

## Task

Prepare for meeting: $ARGUMENTS

## Meeting Preparation Workflow

### Phase 1: Meeting Context Analysis

1. **Meeting Basics**:
   - Purpose/objective of meeting
   - Who called it and why?
   - Agenda (official or implied)
   - Duration and format (in-person, video, phone)

2. **Desired Outcome**:
   - What do YOU want to achieve?
   - What decision needs to be made?
   - What agreement/alignment needed?
   - What information to gather?

3. **Success Criteria**:
   - How will you know meeting was successful?
   - What's the minimum acceptable outcome?
   - What would be ideal outcome?

**Deliverable**: Meeting context brief

**Quality Gate**: Clear purpose and desired outcome?

---

### Phase 2: Participant Research (analyze-agent)

**Research each attendee**:

1. **For Each Participant**:
   - Role and responsibilities
   - Background (LinkedIn, company bio)
   - Their priorities/interests
   - Recent activities (news, posts, achievements)
   - Past interactions with you/company

2. **Stakeholder Mapping**:
   - Decision maker(s)
   - Influencers
   - Supporters vs. skeptics
   - Information needers

3. **What They Care About**:
   - What problems keep them up at night?
   - What metrics matter to them?
   - What's their communication style?
   - What questions might they have?

**Deliverable**: Participant profiles with priorities and concerns

**Quality Gate**: Understand each person's perspective?

---

### Phase 3: Talking Points & Key Messages

**Prepare what to communicate**:

1. **Core Messages** (3-5 key points):
   - What are your main points?
   - Supporting evidence for each
   - Examples/stories to illustrate
   - How they benefit audience

2. **Opening Statement** (30-60 seconds):
   - Grab attention
   - State purpose
   - Preview key points
   - Set expectations

3. **Supporting Points**:
   - Data and facts
   - Case studies
   - Testimonials
   - Relevant research

4. **Closing Statement**:
   - Summarize key points
   - Propose next steps
   - Call to action

**Deliverable**: Talking points document

**Quality Gate**: Messages clear and compelling?

---

### Phase 4: Question Preparation

1. **Questions to Ask**:
   - Information you need
   - Clarifications needed
   - Strategic questions (uncover needs, priorities)
   - Relationship-building questions

2. **Anticipated Questions**:
   - What will they likely ask?
   - Prepare answers for each
   - Know your data/facts
   - Prepare for tough questions

3. **Objection Handling**:
   - What concerns might they raise?
   - How to address each?
   - Evidence to counter objections
   - Fallback positions

**Deliverable**: Question list (to ask + anticipated)

**Quality Gate**: Prepared for likely questions/objections?

---

### Phase 5: Materials & Logistics

**Ensure everything is ready**:

1. **Materials to Prepare**:
   - Presentation slides (if applicable)
   - One-pagers/handouts
   - Proposals/documents to review
   - Data/reports to reference
   - Demo (if applicable)

2. **Pre-Work**:
   - Send agenda (if you're organizing)
   - Share pre-reads
   - Confirm attendance
   - Test technology

3. **Logistics Checklist**:
   - [ ] Meeting link/location confirmed
   - [ ] Calendar invites sent
   - [ ] Materials prepared
   - [ ] Technology tested
   - [ ] Backup plans ready

**Deliverable**: Materials ready, logistics confirmed

**Quality Gate**: Everything prepared and working?

---

### Phase 6: Success Planning

**Plan for follow-through**:

1. **Meeting Flow**:
   - Opening (2 min)
   - Main discussion (bulk of time)
   - Q&A
   - Next steps & closing (5 min)

2. **Note-Taking Plan**:
   - Who takes notes?
   - What to capture (decisions, action items, questions)

3. **Follow-Up Plan**:
   - Thank you email within 24 hours
   - Meeting notes/summary
   - Action items with owners
   - Next meeting scheduling

4. **Contingency Planning**:
   - What if they say no?
   - What if key person doesn't attend?
   - What if running out of time?
   - What if discussion goes off-track?

**Deliverable**: Meeting flow plan and follow-up strategy

**Quality Gate**: Ready to run effective meeting?

## Meeting Type Templates

### Sales Meeting
**Focus**: Understand needs, demonstrate value, advance deal
**Key Questions**: Pain points, decision process, timeline, budget
**Success**: Next step scheduled (demo, proposal, trial)

### Investor Pitch
**Focus**: Traction, market opportunity, team, ask
**Key Questions**: Market size, competitive advantage, use of funds
**Success**: Term sheet or follow-up meeting

### Performance Review
**Focus**: Achievements, growth areas, goals
**Key Questions**: Career aspirations, support needed, feedback
**Success**: Clear goals and development plan

### Negotiation
**Focus**: Understand positions, find mutual benefit
**Key Questions**: Priorities, constraints, alternatives
**Success**: Agreement or clear path to agreement

### Project Kickoff
**Focus**: Align on goals, roles, process
**Key Questions**: Success criteria, constraints, risks
**Success**: Everyone aligned with clear next steps

- [ ] Reviewed all materials
- [ ] Researched participants
- [ ] Prepared talking points
- [ ] Anticipated questions
- [ ] Tested technology

**1 Hour Before**:
- [ ] Reviewed brief
- [ ] Confirmed logistics
- [ ] Gathered materials
- [ ] Cleared calendar buffer

**Right Before**:
- [ ] Deep breath
- [ ] Review key messages
- [ ] Set positive mindset
- [ ] Ready to listen actively

## Quality Standards

- **Well-Researched**: Know participants and context
- **Clear Objectives**: Know what success looks like
- **Prepared Messages**: Can articulate key points clearly
- **Anticipatory**: Ready for likely questions
- **Professional**: Materials polished, logistics handled
- **Follow-Through**: Clear plan for after meeting

**Time Savings**: 1-2 hours → 20-30 minutes  
**Success Rate**: Well-prepared meetings are 3-5x more productive
