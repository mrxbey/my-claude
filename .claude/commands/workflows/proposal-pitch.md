## Multi-Phase Proposal Creation Workflow

### Phase 1: Opportunity Analysis (analyze-agent)

1. **Understand Requirements**:
   - What are they buying/funding?
   - What criteria will they use to decide?
   - What problems are they solving?
   - What's their budget/timeline?

2. **Stakeholder Analysis**:
   - Who are decision makers?
   - What does each stakeholder care about?
   - Who are you competing against?
   - Who influences the decision?

3. **Win Themes**:
   - Why should they choose you?
   - What unique value do you bring?
   - What risks do you mitigate?

**Deliverable**: Opportunity brief with requirements, stakeholders, win themes

**Quality Gate**: Clear understanding of what they want?

---

### Phase 2: Solution Design

1. **Tailor Solution to Requirements**:
   - Match your offering to their needs
   - Highlight relevant capabilities
   - Customize approach for their situation
   - Address all must-have requirements

2. **Differentiation**:
   - What makes your solution unique?
   - Why you vs. competitors?
   - Proof points (case studies, testimonials)

3. **Risk Mitigation**:
   - What concerns might they have?
   - How do you address each concern?
   - What guarantees/safeguards?

**Deliverable**: Customized solution design

**Quality Gate**: Solution clearly addresses their needs?

---

### Phase 3: Value Proposition (analyze-agent + analysis skill)

1. **Quantify Benefits**:
   - ROI calculation
   - Cost savings
   - Revenue impact
   - Time savings
   - Risk reduction

2. **Qualitative Benefits**:
   - Strategic advantages
   - Competitive edge
   - Peace of mind
   - Simplification

3. **Comparison to Alternatives**:
   - Status quo (do nothing)
   - Build in-house
   - Competitor solutions
   - Why your option is best

**Deliverable**: Compelling value proposition with quantified benefits

**Quality Gate**: Value clear and compelling?

---

### Phase 4: Proposal Structure

**Standard Proposal Structure**:

1. **Executive Summary** (1 page):
   - Problem statement
   - Your solution (high-level)
   - Key benefits
   - Investment required
   - Why choose you

2. **Problem/Opportunity**:
   - Current situation
   - Pain points
   - Opportunity cost
   - Why act now

3. **Proposed Solution**:
   - Approach/methodology
   - Scope of work
   - Deliverables
   - Timeline/phases

4. **Why Us**:
   - Relevant experience
   - Case studies/testimonials
   - Team qualifications
   - Differentiators

5. **Implementation Plan**:
   - Project phases
   - Milestones
   - Timeline
   - Success metrics

6. **Investment**:
   - Pricing breakdown
   - Payment terms
   - What's included/excluded
   - Optional add-ons

7. **Next Steps**:
   - Decision timeline
   - How to proceed
   - Contact information
   - Call to action

**Deliverable**: Proposal outline

**Quality Gate**: Structure logical and complete?

---

### Phase 5: Draft Creation

**Write each section**:

1. **Executive Summary** (write LAST):
   - One-page summary of entire proposal
   - Can stand alone
   - Compelling for busy executives

2. **Compelling Opening**:
   - Start with their problem, not your solution
   - Show you understand their situation
   - Create urgency

3. **Evidence-Based Content**:
   - Back claims with data
   - Use specific examples
   - Include testimonials/case studies
   - Show, don't just tell

4. **Visual Elements**:
   - Charts for ROI/savings
   - Timeline/Gantt chart
   - Process diagrams
   - Before/after comparisons

**Deliverable**: Complete draft proposal

**Quality Gate**: All sections written and cohesive?

---

### Phase 6: Compliance Check (docs-agent)

**Use docs-agent** to verify against RFP/requirements:

1. **Requirement Traceability**:
   - Every requirement addressed
   - Cross-reference to sections
   - Nothing missing

2. **Format Compliance**:
   - Page limits respected
   - Required sections included
   - Specified format followed

3. **Submission Requirements**:
   - Deadline met
   - Required signatures/forms
   - Delivery method correct

**Deliverable**: Compliance checklist

**Quality Gate**: All requirements met?

---

### Phase 7: Polish & Proofread (proofread-agent)

1. **Proofread thoroughly**
2. **Professional formatting**
3. **Consistent terminology**
4. **Visual polish**

**Deliverable**: Publication-ready proposal

**Quality Gate**: Error-free and professional?

## Proposal Templates by Type

### Sales Proposal
**Focus**: ROI, time-to-value, risk mitigation
**Length**: 5-15 pages
**Tone**: Professional, confident, benefit-focused

### Grant Application
**Focus**: Mission alignment, impact, sustainability
**Length**: Varies by funder
**Tone**: Formal, evidence-based, mission-driven

### Partnership Proposal
**Focus**: Mutual benefit, strategic fit, win-win
**Length**: 3-10 pages
**Tone**: Collaborative, strategic, relationship-focused

### RFP Response
**Focus**: Compliance, qualifications, competitive pricing
**Length**: Per RFP specs
**Tone**: Professional, detailed, requirement-focused

---

## Quality Standards

- **Customer-Focused**: About them, not you
- **Evidence-Based**: Claims backed by data/proof
- **Clear Value**: ROI and benefits obvious
- **Professional**: Error-free, well-designed
- **Compliant**: Meets all requirements
- **Compelling**: Makes the decision easy

**Win Rate Improvement**: Well-structured proposals win 2-3x more often

**Time Savings**: 6-8 hours → 2-3 hours
