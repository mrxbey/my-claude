## 🚀 Quick Start Examples

### I need to research something

```bash
/workflows/research-synthesis "What are the best CRM platforms for B2B SaaS?"
```

**What happens:**
- 6-phase systematic research
- Evidence table with sources
- Synthesis with confidence levels
- Comprehensive report with citations

**Time:** 8 hours → 1 hour (8-12x faster)

---

### I need to write content

```bash
/workflows/content-creation "Blog post: 10 Ways AI Transforms Customer Service"
```

**What happens:**
- Audience analysis
- Research & outline
- Draft → style refinement → proofread
- Brand compliance check
- Publication-ready article

**Time:** 2 hours → 30 minutes (4-5x faster)

---

### I need to send an important email

```bash
/workflows/professional-email "Request budget approval from CFO for Q3 marketing spend"
```

**What happens:**
- Context analysis (relationship, urgency, culture)
- Tone selection (formal, diplomatic, direct)
- Email composition with clear action items
- Clarity check & proofread

**Time:** 30 minutes → 5 minutes (6x faster)

---

### I need to analyze competitors

```bash
/workflows/competitive-analysis "AI-powered customer service platforms"
```

**What happens:**
- Competitor identification & profiling
- Feature/pricing comparison matrices
- SWOT analysis
- Strategic positioning map
- Actionable recommendations

**Time:** 12 hours → 2-3 hours (4-6x faster)

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Combine Workflows
Workflows can work together:

```
1. Research topic → /workflows/research-synthesis
2. Use research for blog → /workflows/content-creation
3. Email to promote blog → /workflows/professional-email
```

### Customize for Your Needs
- Add context in your request
- Specify tone/style preferences
- Mention constraints (time, length, audience)
- Reference existing materials

### Quality Gates Built-In
Every workflow includes validation checkpoints:
- ✅ Requirements clear?
- ✅ Evidence sufficient?
- ✅ Quality standards met?
- ✅ Ready to ship?
