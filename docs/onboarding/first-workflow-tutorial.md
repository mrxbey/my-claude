# 🎓 Your First Workflow: Step-by-Step Tutorial

**Goal**: Complete your first workflow successfully in < 3 minutes and understand how MyClaude works.

**What you'll learn**:

**Time**: 2-3 minutes

---

## Step 1: Copy This Exact Command

We're going to use the **Professional Email** workflow because it's fast and immediately useful.

**Copy this command exactly** (click the copy button):

```bash
/workflows:professional-email "Request meeting with my manager to discuss Q1 goals and priorities"
```

**What this does**: Creates a professional email requesting a meeting with your manager about Q1 planning.

---

## Step 2: Paste and Run

1. **Open your terminal** where MyClaude is installed
2. **Paste the command** you just copied
3. **Press Enter**

**What happens next**: You'll see MyClaude working through several phases:

```
🔄 Phase 1: Understanding Context...
🔄 Phase 2: Analyzing Tone and Audience...
🔄 Phase 3: Drafting Email...
🔄 Phase 4: Polishing and Formatting...
✅ Email Draft Complete!
```

**Time**: ~30 seconds

---

## Step 3: Review the Output

MyClaude will generate a complete email with:

### Email Structure
```
Subject: Meeting Request - Q1 Goals and Priorities Discussion

Hi [Manager Name],

I'd like to schedule time with you to discuss our Q1 goals and
priorities. I want to ensure my work this quarter aligns with team
objectives and company priorities.

Would you have 30 minutes available this week or early next week?
I'm flexible with timing and happy to work around your schedule.

I've been thinking about [specific area] and would value your input
on priorities and focus areas for the quarter.

Please let me know what works best for you.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**What to notice**:
- ✅ **Professional tone** - Appropriate for manager communication
- ✅ **Clear purpose** - Meeting request with specific topic
- ✅ **Action item** - Asks for availability
- ✅ **Context** - Explains why the meeting matters
- ✅ **Flexibility** - Shows respect for manager's time

## Step 4: Understand What Just Happened

**The workflow followed a systematic process:**

### Phase 1: Context Understanding
- Analyzed: "Request meeting with my manager"
- Identified: Upward communication (employee → manager)
- Determined: Professional but collaborative tone needed

### Phase 2: Audience Analysis
- Audience: Direct manager
- Relationship: Professional, existing relationship
- Formality: Professional but not overly formal
- Goal: Request meeting, explain value

### Phase 3: Content Drafting
- Created subject line
- Wrote opening with clear request
- Added context and value proposition
- Included specific call-to-action
- Professional closing

### Phase 4: Polish
- Checked tone consistency
- Verified clarity
- Ensured action items are clear
- Formatted for readability

**Result**: Professional email in 30 seconds vs 25 minutes to write manually

**Time Saved**: 24.5 minutes! ⏱️

## Step 5: Customize for Your Situation

**The generated email is a starting point.** Here's how to customize:

### Personalize the Details
Replace placeholders with your specifics:
- `[Manager Name]` → Your actual manager's name
- `[specific area]` → Specific project or priority you want to discuss
- `[Your Name]` → Your name

### Adjust the Tone
The workflow chose a professional tone, but you can request adjustments:

**More casual** (if you have that relationship):
```bash
/workflows:professional-email "Request meeting with my manager to discuss Q1 goals - keep it casual and friendly"
```

**More formal** (if needed):
```bash
/workflows:professional-email "Request meeting with executive sponsor for quarterly business review - very formal tone"
```

**Add urgency**:
```bash
/workflows:professional-email "Urgent: Request meeting with manager about client escalation - need to meet this week"
```

### Modify the Content
After reviewing the output, you can:
1. **Use it as-is** if it's perfect
2. **Edit specific sections** (add details, change wording)
3. **Combine with your own additions** (attach documents, add bullet points)

**The workflow gives you 90% of the work done.** You provide the final 10% of personalization.

## Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

### ❌ Mistake 1: Too Vague
**Bad**: `/workflows:professional-email "write an email"`

**Why it's bad**: Workflow doesn't know who, what, why, or tone

**Good**: `/workflows:professional-email "Request budget increase from CFO for marketing campaign"`

**Why it's good**: Clear recipient (CFO), purpose (budget request), context (marketing campaign)

---

### ❌ Mistake 2: No Context
**Bad**: `/workflows:professional-email "email to John"`

**Why it's bad**: Workflow doesn't know relationship, purpose, or content

**Good**: `/workflows:professional-email "Thank my colleague John for helping with the presentation"`

**Why it's good**: Relationship (colleague), purpose (thank you), context (presentation help)

---

### ❌ Mistake 3: Expecting Perfection
**Bad**: Thinking the output will be 100% ready to send without any review

**Why it's bad**: The workflow doesn't know your specific details, names, dates, etc.

**Good**: Expecting 90% complete output that needs your final 10% of personalization

**Why it's good**: Realistic expectation, workflow handles structure/tone, you add specifics

## Troubleshooting

### "The tone doesn't feel right"
**Solution**: Add tone guidance to your command:
- `"... - use casual friendly tone"`
- `"... - very formal executive tone"`
- `"... - warm and appreciative tone"`

### "The email is too long/short"
**Solution**: Specify length:
- `"... - keep it brief, 3 sentences max"`
- `"... - comprehensive email with details"`

### "It's missing specific details"
**Solution**: The workflow can't know your specific details. Add them after generation:
- Company names
- Dates and times
- Project specifics
- Personal references

### "I want a different structure"
**Solution**: Specify structure in your command:
- `"... - bullet point format"`
- `"... - include executive summary at top"`
- `"... - start with specific ask, then context"`
