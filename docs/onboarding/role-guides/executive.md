## 💡 Pro Tips for Executive Use Cases

### Strategic Intelligence Tips
1. **Update quarterly**: Markets evolve, refresh your understanding
2. **Focus on what matters**: Threats, opportunities, strategic moves
3. **Share selectively**: Not all intelligence is for all audiences
4. **Act on insights**: Intelligence has no value without action

### Communication Tips
1. **Be brief**: Executives are busy, respect their time
2. **Lead with conclusion**: What do you need them to know/do?
3. **Use data**: "Based on analysis showing..."
4. **Clear CTAs**: What's the next step?

### Delegation Tips
1. **Use workflows for first draft**: Then add your perspective
2. **Have EA customize**: Workflow drafts, EA polishes with your voice
3. **Review efficiently**: Focus on strategy, not wordsmithing

## ❓ FAQ for Executives

**Q: Can I delegate this to my team?**
A: Yes! Your team runs workflows, you review and add executive perspective.

**Q: Is the intelligence reliable?**
A: Workflows synthesize from credible sources with citations. Verify critical decisions with advisors.

**Q: What about confidential strategy?**
A: Use workflows for market research and drafting. Don't input confidential information.

**Q: How do I integrate with my EA?**
A: EA runs workflows for drafts, you review and approve. Saves EA time too.

**Q: What's the ROI for an executive?**
A: 40+ hours/month saved. If your time is worth $500/hour, that's $20K+/month value.
