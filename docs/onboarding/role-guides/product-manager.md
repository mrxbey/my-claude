## 💡 Pro Tips for PM Use Cases

### Research Tips
1. **Start broad, narrow down**: General trend research → specific feature research
2. **Triangulate sources**: Don't rely on one source, synthesize multiple
3. **Save your searches**: Keep research for future reference
4. **Share with team**: Research helps everyone make better decisions

### Competitive Analysis Tips
1. **Update quarterly**: Competitors evolve, refresh your intel
2. **Focus on strategy, not just features**: Why are they doing this?
3. **Look for gaps**: What aren't competitors doing well?
4. **Use for positioning**: Direct input to marketing messaging

### Meeting Prep Tips
1. **Know your audience**: Executives want different info than engineers
2. **Lead with recommendation**: Don't make them guess what you want
3. **Have data ready**: "Based on research showing..."
4. **Prepare for no**: What if they reject your proposal?

## ❓ FAQ for PMs

**Q: Will this replace customer conversations?**
A: No! Workflows handle secondary research. You still need to talk to customers for primary insights.

**Q: How do I cite research in PRDs?**
A: Workflow provides sources. Include them in your PRD references section.

**Q: Can I use this for technical research?**
A: Yes! Research workflow handles technical topics well. Great for technology evaluations.

**Q: What about proprietary data?**
A: Workflows use public information. Combine with your internal data for complete picture.

**Q: How often should I update competitive analysis?**
A: Quarterly minimum. Monthly for fast-moving markets.
