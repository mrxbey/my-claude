## 💡 Pro Tips for Sales Use Cases

### Meeting Prep Tips
1. **Research stakeholders on LinkedIn first**: Feed their background into workflow
2. **Include recent company news**: Shows you've done homework
3. **Prepare for ghost**: Have "no response" follow-up ready
4. **Update after meeting**: Note what worked for next time

### Proposal Tips
1. **Start with executive summary**: Busy executives may only read first page
2. **Show ROI in months, not years**: Faster payback = easier approval
3. **Include implementation timeline**: Reduces "how long will this take?" objection
4. **Add case studies**: Social proof from similar companies
5. **Version control**: Save versions as you customize for feedback

### Email Tips
1. **One clear CTA**: Don't give them 3 options, make it easy to say yes
2. **Executive = short**: C-level gets 3 paragraphs max
3. **Prospect = value-first**: Lead with their pain, not your features
4. **Follow-up = reference specific**: "Per our conversation about X..."

## ❓ FAQ for Sales

**Q: Will prospects know I used AI?**
A: Not if you customize and personalize. Think of it as having a sales engineer who drafts everything for you to polish.

**Q: Can I use this for RFPs?**
A: Yes! Proposal workflow is perfect for RFPs. You still need to answer specific questions, but the structure and prose are handled.

**Q: What about legal review?**
A: Always have legal review contracts and agreements. Workflows handle business case and narrative, not legal terms.

**Q: Does this work for complex enterprise sales?**
A: Especially well! Complex deals need better preparation and proposals. Workflows give you time to focus on relationship building.

**Q: How do I track which workflows help close deals?**
A: Note in CRM when you use workflows. Track win rate for deals with workflow-assisted proposals vs without.
