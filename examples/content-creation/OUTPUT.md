## The Five Principles of Effective AI Workflows

If you're considering implementing AI workflows, here's what actually works:

### 1. Systematic Process > Raw Speed

Bad approach: "AI, make this faster"
Good approach: Multi-phase process with quality gates at each step

The workflows that deliver real value have 5-7 distinct phases (research → outline → draft → refine → proofread → compliance → polish), not one "generate content" step.

### 2. Context-Aware > Generic

Bad approach: Same workflow for everyone
Good approach: Adapts based on your role, audience, brand voice, company culture

A workflow should know that emails to your CFO need different tone than emails to your direct reports.

### 3. Quality Gates > Automation

Bad approach: Fully autonomous (no human oversight)
Good approach: Validation checkpoints where you review before continuing

The best workflows ask: "Does this research question make sense before I spend 30 minutes researching?" not just "Here's your research report."

### 4. Composition > Isolation

Bad approach: Isolated workflows that don't connect
Good approach: Workflows that build on each other

Example: Research workflow → feeds into content workflow → feeds into email workflow for promotion. Connected, not siloed.

### 5. Evidence-Based > Black Box

Bad approach: AI gives you an answer, you trust it
Good approach: AI shows its work—sources, reasoning, confidence levels

You should see exactly which sources support which claims, with credibility ratings.
