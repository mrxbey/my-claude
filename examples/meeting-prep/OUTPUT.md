## Your Strategic Positioning

### Core Message (15 seconds)

"We're losing $400K annually to preventable customer churn because we can't identify at-risk accounts early enough. This $250K investment pays for itself in 7 months through churn reduction, and gives our product team the insights they need to improve retention long-term. The risk is waiting—every quarter we delay costs us $80K in preventable churn."

### Three Key Talking Points

#### 1. **The Problem Is Costing Us Money** (Churn Focus)
- Customer churn up 12% YoY (from 8% to 9%)
- At $50K average customer lifetime value = $400K annual loss
- Current tools are reactive (we learn customers are unhappy when they cancel)
- Root cause: Can't identify at-risk accounts early enough

**Evidence**:
- Q3 churn analysis: 73% of churned customers showed early warning signs we didn't detect
- CS team spends 15 hours/week manually pulling reports
- Average time to detect at-risk customer: 45 days (too late to save them)

#### 2. **The ROI Is Conservative and Provable** (CFO Focus)
- **Investment**: $250K implementation + $60K/year subscription = $310K Year 1
- **Returns Year 1**:
  - Churn reduction: 9% → 7.5% = $320K saved (conservative 15% improvement)
  - CS efficiency: 15 hours/week → 5 hours = $180K annual value
  - **Total**: $500K return on $310K investment = 1.6x ROI Year 1
- **Payback period**: 7 months

**Why Conservative**:
- Used 15% churn improvement (vendor case studies show 20-30%)
- Only counted CS time savings, not product team benefits
- Didn't include revenue upside from better product decisions

#### 3. **The Implementation Risk Is Manageable** (CTO + CFO Focus)
- **Vendor**: Leading platform with 200+ enterprise customers
- **Integration**: API-based, 8-12 week implementation (vendor handles 80%)
- **Engineering time**: 40 hours total (1 engineer, 1 week spread over 3 months)
- **Pilot option**: $50K for 6-week pilot to validate ROI before full commitment
- **Unlike CRM**: No data migration, no process re-engineering, additive not replacement

## Anticipated Questions & Prepared Answers

### From Sarah (CFO)

**Q: "How do I know we'll actually achieve this ROI? These projections always look great on paper."**

**A**: "Great question. Three things give me confidence:

First, I used the most conservative estimates I could find. Vendor case studies show 20-30% churn improvement, I modeled 15%. If we only achieve half of what I projected, it still pays back in 14 months.

Second, we can validate this with a 6-week pilot for $50K. We'd test with 100 customers, measure early warning signal accuracy, and only proceed to full implementation if we see the data quality we need.

Third, the CS efficiency gains are guaranteed—even if we don't reduce churn at all, we save 10 hours/week of manual reporting. That's $90K/year minimum return.

I'm proposing we do the pilot first. If the results aren't there, we've only spent $50K to learn that, instead of $250K."

---

**Q: "Why now? Can this wait until Q2 when we have more budget certainty?"**

**A**: "I understand the timing concern. Here's the cost of waiting:

We're losing roughly $30K per month to preventable churn based on Q3 data. Waiting until Q2 means 3+ months of additional losses = $90K minimum.

Plus, the budget cycle for Q2 doesn't start until March, and implementation takes 8-12 weeks. So realistically we wouldn't see benefits until June—6 months from now.

The opportunity cost of waiting is higher than the budget risk of proceeding.

That said, the pilot option addresses this. We could start with $50K now, validate the approach, and make the full budget decision in January with real data. That way we're learning in Q4 instead of waiting."

---

**Q: "We just got burned on the CRM implementation. How is this different?"**

**A**: "I thought about this a lot, because I don't want to repeat that experience. Here are the key differences:

**CRM issues**:
- Custom development required (expensive, risky)
- Full data migration (messy, error-prone)
- Process re-engineering (change management nightmare)
- Replaced existing system (high switching cost)

**This platform**:
- API-based integration, vendor-managed (they've done this 200+ times)
- No data migration, connects to existing systems
- Additive tool, doesn't replace anything
- Vendor provides full implementation support and training

Plus, Michael, I'd like your input on the technical implementation plan. If you see risks I'm missing, let's address them upfront. I don't want to commit to something your team can't support.

And finally, the pilot option lets us test the implementation approach before full commitment."

**Technology risk**: That's exactly why I'm proposing the pilot. We test with 100 customers for 6 weeks, validate the accuracy of the insights, and make sure it integrates properly. If it doesn't work, we've learned that for $50K instead of $250K.
