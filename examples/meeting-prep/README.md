# Meeting Preparation Workflow Example

## What This Example Shows

- **Input**: Basic meeting context (who, what, when, why)
- **Process**: 6-phase systematic preparation workflow
- **Output**: Comprehensive meeting brief with strategy and materials
- **Time Saved**: 2 hours → 30 minutes = **4x faster**

## Workflow Execution

**Phase 1: Meeting Context Analysis** (5 min) - Understanding objectives and stakeholders
**Phase 2: Participant Research** (8 min) - Profiling attendees and their priorities
**Phase 3: Strategic Preparation** (7 min) - Key messages, talking points, positioning
**Phase 4: Q&A Anticipation** (6 min) - Predicting questions and preparing answers
**Phase 5: Materials & Logistics** (3 min) - Checklist of what you need
**Phase 6: Success Planning** (1 min) - Success criteria and follow-up strategy

**Total**: ~30 minutes | **Manual**: 2 hours | **Savings**: 4x
