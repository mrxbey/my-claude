# Professional Email Workflow Example

This example demonstrates the `/workflows/professional-email` workflow in action.

## What This Example Shows

- **Input**: Simple email context
- **Process**: 6-phase email composition workflow
- **Output**: 5 professionally-crafted emails with different tones
- **Time Saved**: 30 minutes → 5 minutes per email = **6x faster**

## The Challenge

Writing important emails is time-consuming and stressful:
- ❌ Agonizing over tone (too formal? too casual?)
- ❌ Multiple drafts and revisions
- ❌ Uncertainty about effectiveness
- ❌ 20-30 minutes per important email

**Daily impact**: 5 important emails × 30 min = **2.5 hours lost every day**

## The Solution

Professional email workflow provides:
- ✅ Context-aware tone selection
- ✅ Clear structure and messaging
- ✅ Appropriate formality level
- ✅ Perfect first draft in 5 minutes

## Workflow Execution

### Phase 1: Context Analysis (1 minute)
- Analyzed recipient relationship (CFO = executive, formal)
- Assessed urgency (budget approval deadline)
- Considered organizational culture (professional, data-driven)
- Identified key constraints (busy executive, needs quick decision)

### Phase 2: Goal Identification (1 minute)
- Primary purpose: Request budget approval
- Desired outcome: Approved by Friday
- Success criteria: Clear yes/no response with timeline

### Phase 3: Tone Selection (1 minute)
- Formality: Professional-formal (executive communication)
- Approach: Direct (clear ask, time-sensitive)
- Tone markers: Respectful, concise, data-backed

### Phase 4: Email Composition (1.5 minutes)
- Subject line: Clear and actionable
- Purpose stated in first sentence
- Supporting data in brief paragraph
- Clear ask with deadline
- Professional close

### Phase 5: Clarity & Brevity Check (30 seconds)
- Verified one main ask (budget approval)
- Removed unnecessary words (180 words → 142 words)
- Enhanced scannability (bullets for key data)

### Phase 6: Proofread & Polish (30 seconds)
- Grammar check
- Tone verification
- Completeness check

**Total Time**: ~5 minutes
**Manual Time**: 25-30 minutes
**Time Savings**: 6x faster
