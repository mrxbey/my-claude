# Proposal & Pitch Input

```bash
/workflows/proposal-pitch "RFP Response: AI Customer Support Implementation for TechRetail Corp"
```

## RFP Context

**Company**: TechRetail Corp
**Industry**: E-commerce & Consumer Electronics
**Size**: 500 employees, $50M annual revenue
**Current Situation**: Receiving 15,000+ customer support tickets/month, average response time 12 hours, customer satisfaction score 6.5/10

**RFP Purpose**: Seeking vendor to implement AI-powered customer support system to reduce response times and improve customer satisfaction

## Key Requirements from RFP

1. **Reduce average response time from 12 hours to < 2 hours**
2. **Handle 70%+ of common inquiries without human intervention**
3. **Improve customer satisfaction score from 6.5/10 to 8.5/10**
4. **Integrate with existing Zendesk ticketing system**
5. **Maintain brand voice and tone consistency**
6. **Provide analytics dashboard for support team**
7. **Implementation timeline: 8-12 weeks**
8. **Budget range: $75K-$150K initial + ongoing costs**

## Constraints & Considerations

- Must comply with GDPR and CCPA (international customers)
- Support team of 12 people concerned about job security
- Peak support hours: 9am-5pm EST weekdays
- 30% of tickets are technical troubleshooting
- 40% are order status/shipping questions
- 20% are returns/refunds
- 10% are product recommendations

## Decision Criteria (from RFP)

1. **Relevant experience** (30%) - Similar implementations, references
2. **Technical approach** (25%) - Architecture, integration plan
3. **ROI & value proposition** (20%) - Cost savings, efficiency gains
4. **Implementation plan** (15%) - Timeline, risk mitigation
5. **Ongoing support** (10%) - Maintenance, training, updates

## Additional Context Provided

"We're a fast-growing e-commerce company and our support team is drowning. We need a solution that helps our team be more efficient, not replace them. Our customers value personal service, so the AI needs to feel helpful, not robotic. We've tried chatbots before and they were terrible - customers hated them. We need something better."
