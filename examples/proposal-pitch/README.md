# Proposal & Pitch Workflow Example

## What This Example Shows

- **Input**: RFP or opportunity context
- **Process**: 7-phase proposal development workflow
- **Output**: Professional, winning proposal
- **Time Saved**: 8 hours → 2-3 hours = **3-4x faster**

## Workflow Execution

**Phase 1: Opportunity Analysis** (25 min) - Understanding requirements and stakeholders
**Phase 2: Solution Design** (30 min) - Tailoring solution to needs
**Phase 3: Value Proposition** (25 min) - Quantifying benefits and ROI
**Phase 4: Proposal Structure** (20 min) - Organizing logically
**Phase 5: Draft Creation** (45 min) - Writing compelling proposal
**Phase 6: Compliance Check** (15 min) - Verifying all requirements met
**Phase 7: Polish & Proofread** (20 min) - Final professional polish

**Total**: ~3 hours | **Manual**: 8+ hours | **Savings**: 3-4x

[OUTPUT.md](OUTPUT.md) shows a professional proposal including:
- Executive summary
