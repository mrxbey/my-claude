## What is CloudSync Pro?

CloudSync Pro is an enterprise-grade collaboration platform designed for modern, distributed workforces. It combines real-time document editing, video conferencing, and intelligent task management into a single, unified experience.

### Key Features

**Real-Time Collaboration**
Work together on documents, spreadsheets, and presentations simultaneously. See your teammates' cursors and changes as they happen, with zero lag.

**Smart Notifications**
Our AI-powered notification system learns your work patterns and only alerts you when it matters. Say goodbye to notification fatigue.

**Enterprise Security**
Bank-level encryption, GDPR compliance, and SOC 2 certification ensure your data stays secure. We never sell or share your information with third parties.

**Seamless Integration**
CloudSync Pro integrates with your existing tools: Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, and 50+ other apps.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I migrate from my current collaboration tool?**
A: Yes! We offer free migration assistance for Business and Enterprise customers. Our team will help you transfer documents, user accounts, and settings.

**Q: What happens to my data if I cancel?**
A: You can export all your data in standard formats (PDF, DOCX, CSV) before canceling. We retain data for 30 days after cancellation, then permanently delete it.
