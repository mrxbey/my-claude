## Notes & Decisions

### Design Decisions
- Use consistent README/INPUT/OUTPUT structure across all examples
- Show realistic business scenarios (not generic/obvious AI)
- Include enough detail to prove value without overwhelming
- Focus on actionability (users can imagine using this output)

### Quality Standards
- Professional writing (no fluff, no marketing hype)
- Accurate time savings (based on realistic manual effort)
- Realistic scenarios (real companies, real situations where possible)
- Actionable outputs (users could actually use these)

### Risks & Mitigation
- **Risk**: Examples feel generic/AI-generated
  - **Mitigation**: Use specific details, real company examples, authentic tone
- **Risk**: Time savings seem exaggerated
  - **Mitigation**: Conservative estimates, explain manual process clearly
- **Risk**: Examples too long/overwhelming
  - **Mitigation**: Balance detail with scannability, use formatting
